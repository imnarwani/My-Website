What I've built

I own products end to end: discovery, PRD, delivery, launch and adoption tracking. I work daily with Engineering, Design, Compliance and Operations.

UPI intent deposits and withdrawals

Compliant payment flows built to NPCI standards, with validations, real-time status tracking and clear error handling. Deposit transactions grew 150% and deposit value 25%.

KYC, re-KYC and bank verification

Redesigned onboarding and verification journeys with structured requirements. Conversion rose 30% and funnel drop-off fell.

QuickTrade

Led the trading workflow redesign across order execution, trade limits and latency. It contributed a 15% revenue increase.

Multichain crypto send and receive

Cross-network transfers across multiple blockchains, improving reliability and scalability for millions of users.

Compliance and risk tooling

Internal platforms for user blacklisting, lien management, compliance activity management and circuit limits. They replace manual review with real-time controls.

Customer statements and DPDP consent

Rebuilt fiat and crypto reporting for trade bifurcation, audit trails and tax statements, plus consent and cookie management for India's DPDP law. Fewer support escalations followed.

Roadmaps built around regulation

I prioritise backlogs against FIU, NPCI and RBI requirements alongside business goals and technical feasibility.
